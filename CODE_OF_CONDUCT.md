# Code of Conduct

Huh → Oh is for people learning things that feel hard at first. The whole point is the moment "huh?" turns into "oh!". Everyone here should feel safe asking the "huh?".

## Expected

- Be kind, patient and specific, especially with beginners. There are no stupid questions here.
- Critique the work, not the person. Say what's wrong and how it could be better.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming.
- Give credit. Say where an idea or a fix came from.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

All project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anything else run in the project's name.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/shriyansh24). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove content, or ban a contributor, temporarily or permanently, depending on what happened.
