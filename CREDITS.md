# Credits

## Everyone

This project was made with a lot of help from AI models. Those models learned from basically every page, post, paper, forum answer, textbook, lecture, commit message and 2 a.m. Stack Overflow comment that people have ever put on the internet.

So the honest credits list is: **everyone.**

To every teacher who explained it a third time. To everyone who wrote a tutorial nobody thanked them for. To the people who answered a stranger's question in a forum in 2009 and never found out it helped. To every open-source maintainer who fixed a bug on a weekend. To everyone who wrote documentation, and everyone who complained until the documentation got better. To every student whose "huh?" made someone explain it properly.

You are in here somewhere. Thank you.

*To those who came before me, I bow.*

*To those who come after: here, I hold up my small part of the torch, to light a little of the way ahead, the way countless others like me are doing right now, and have done before.*

*For the love of the game.*
